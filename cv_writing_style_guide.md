# CV Writing Style Guide

How this CV is written, and how to keep writing it. Reference examples pulled directly from `Cosmin_Turculeanu_PDF_CV.html`.

## The core rule

If a sentence could appear in a generic LinkedIn "proud to announce" post, rewrite it. Every claim needs a mechanism (how) and, where possible, a specific number (how much).

## Banned words and phrases

Never use: results-driven, passionate about, proven track record, leverage (as a verb), synergy, dynamic, detail-oriented, team player, go-getter, innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, spearheaded, utilize, responsible for, extensive experience, hit the ground running, wear many hats, robust (describing your own work), game-changer, best-in-class.

Also avoid stacked-adjective openers like "Highly motivated, results-oriented professional with a passion for..."

## Patterns to follow, with real examples from this CV

**1. Causal chain: problem found → action → measured effect**
> "Identified workflow risks causing ~10% excess in monthly operational costs, then fixed them"

Not: "Optimized operational processes to reduce costs."

**2. Mechanism over outcome — say *how*, not just *that***
> "Built n8n workflow automation to rebalance workloads dynamically across 25+ desks, removed manual intervention entirely from operational routing"

Not: "Improved operational efficiency through automation."

**3. Honest about constraints, even in your own favor's roles**
> "...working within approval chains as Supervisor, changes had to go through sign-off, which limited pace but built the operational foundation carried forward into the Manager role"

This kind of admission makes the surrounding claims more credible, not less impressive.

**4. Specific, non-round numbers**
Used throughout: 95%, 30-35%, 25+, ~10%, 8%, 80%, 15%. Never "50%" or "2x" or "significantly." Round numbers read as guessed; odd numbers read as measured.

**5. Verb-first, no "I," no "responsible for"**
> "Joined as one of two people building the automation function from scratch, now owning the full delivery cycle from intake to sign-off."

**6. State the unfinished/boring truth plainly**
> "Currently in security audit phase before release, demo available in portfolio"

Not hyped as a launch, not hidden — just stated.

**7. No self-praise adjectives on your own output**
The CV never calls its own systems "innovative," "seamless," "powerful." The mechanism and the number do that work instead.

**8. Plain scope-setting sentences**
> "Working in an Agile framework, now owning the full delivery cycle, running 2-week sprints and scrum ceremonies with engineers and analysts. Manage backlog prioritization by business value."

Short, factual, no inflation.

**9. Slightly informal, direct closing lines are fine**
> "Looking for roles where direct contribution is part of the job."

Reads as a real preference, not a mission statement or objective-statement cliché.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] No banned words anywhere
- [ ] Every bullet has a concrete mechanism (the *how*)
- [ ] Numbers are specific, not round, where a real number exists
- [ ] No adjectives praising your own work ("innovative," "seamless," "robust")
- [ ] No "responsible for" / no throat-clearing intro sentences
- [ ] Constraints/limits stated honestly where relevant, not smoothed over
- [ ] Read it aloud — if it sounds like a LinkedIn post, rewrite it
